MEET JULIA

Julia Bliss is an internationally recognized DJ, producer, and entrepreneur who has built her career from the ground up — from Siberia to some of the biggest stages in India and beyond.

A regular performer at major festivals including Sunburn, Julia has spent years developing her sound, performing around the world, and navigating the realities of building a career in electronic music.
